01 · Introduction

A cup that pays you back

Chipt is a conceptual mobile app designed to encourage reusable cup adoption in coffee shops. The idea explores how loyalty rewards and simple interactions can motivate people to return reusable cups instead of reaching for disposable ones.

The project focuses on one seamless mobile experience that connects buying coffee with sustainability driven rewards: order in a Chipt cup, tap it with your phone, earn points, return the cup, and redeem rewards on the next purchase.

Chipt is built for both sides of the counter. For customers (B2C) it turns the sustainable choice into rewards. For coffee shops (B2B) it is a retention engine: the cup must come back to the shop to unlock the discount on the next coffee, so every cup sent out is a customer coming back in.

02 · The problem

Convenience beats conscience

Disposable coffee cups are everywhere because they are effortless. Even when sustainable alternatives exist, people avoid them for three very human reasons:

REASON 01

Reusable options require extra effort from the customer.

REASON 02

There is no immediate reward for doing the right thing.

REASON 03

The process feels slower than just grabbing a disposable cup.

So the challenge was clear: design a solution that benefits the environment and the coffee vendors at the same time, without asking the customer to work for it.

03 · Opportunity

Where sustainability meets retention

The insight that shaped the product: the customer's problem and the business's problem can solve each other. A returned cup earns the customer a discount on the next coffee, and that discount is claimed at the shop. The cup itself pulls people back through the door.

1
Behavioural

Customer needs

Customers want to be sustainable but struggle with the inconvenience. They need frictionless incentives to change habits.

2
Economic

Business needs

Coffee shops operate on thin margins and need repeat customers. With Chipt, every cup returned to the shop is a guaranteed second visit and a discounted next purchase.

3
Strategic

The product bridge

Technology connects the two. By digitising the reusable cup, a good deed becomes a measurable reward loop.

04 · Solution

A circular system, not just an app

Chipt is a digitally enabled circular system that replaces single use cups with smart, trackable assets. It bridges the gap between sustainability and convenience.

NFC embedded reusable cups
Tap to register technology
Loyalty points earned automatically
Rewards redeemed on the next purchase
Zero waste circular ecosystem

09 · Persona

Designing with purpose

Aarav Sharma
Office professional · 26 · Daily coffee buyer
Primary goals 72%
Core needs 80%
Common pain points 25%

As a regular coffee buyer, Aarav wants a quick and convenient way to purchase coffee while also earning rewards. He prefers simple systems that let him return reusable cups, collect loyalty points and redeem them easily, without disrupting his daily routine.

"I get coffee almost every day. If returning my cup earns rewards and helps reduce waste, it feels like a win for both me and the environment."Aarav Sharma
